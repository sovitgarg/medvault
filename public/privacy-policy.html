🏥 MedVault Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 19, 2025

Privacy Statement: MedVault runs entirely in your browser. We never see, store, or access your medical documents.

What is MedVault?

MedVault is a privacy-first web application that helps medical professionals organize documents in their Google Drive using camera capture and file uploads.

Zero-Access Architecture

MedVault is built with a zero-backend, client-side-only architecture:

  • No backend server - the app runs entirely in your browser
  • No database - we don't store any user data
  • No file storage - all files go directly to your Google Drive
  • No analytics - we don't track your usage

Google Drive Access

We request minimal Google Drive permissions:

  • drive.file - Create and manage files you create with MedVault
  • drive.metadata.readonly - View folder names and metadata
  • drive.folders - Create and organize folders

Important: We do NOT use broad Drive access. We cannot read your existing files or access files not created through MedVault.

Data Flow

When you use MedVault:

  1. You authenticate with Google OAuth (handled by Google)
  2. Your browser receives an access token (stored in session storage)
  3. When you upload a file, it goes directly from your browser to Google Drive
  4. MedVault never touches, sees, or stores your file content

Session Storage

We use browser session storage (not cookies) to store:

  • Google OAuth access token (deleted when you close the browser)
  • Your basic profile info (name, email, profile picture)

This data is only stored in your browser and is never sent to any server.

HIPAA Compliance

MedVault is designed with HIPAA-compliant architecture in mind:

  • No PHI (Protected Health Information) is ever transmitted to or stored by MedVault
  • All data stays in your Google Drive account
  • No business associate agreement is needed because we never access PHI

Note: Users are responsible for ensuring their Google Drive account meets their organization's HIPAA requirements.

Third-Party Services

MedVault relies on:

  • Google OAuth & Drive API - For authentication and file storage
  • Cloudflare Pages - For hosting static files (no logging)

Your Rights

You have full control:

  • Revoke MedVault's access anytime via Google Account Permissions
  • All files remain in your Google Drive after revocation
  • Delete session data by logging out or closing your browser

Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
